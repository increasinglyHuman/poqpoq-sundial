Sundial for three.js

Paged (virtual) shadow maps for the sun on WebGPURenderer: shadow depth is drawn once into 128×128 pages at the resolution the screen needs, and kept until something moves.

This demo needs WebGPU

Sundial runs on three.js WebGPURenderer with its WebGPU backend, which this browser does not provide. Try Chrome or Edge 113+, Safari 26, or Firefox 141+ on Windows. Behind this note is a screenshot of the live demo.
